# Vani — Learning Without the Barriers

> **What if someone wants to learn, but the technology required to learn is exactly what they don't have?**

Vani is a voice-first learning assistant that lets people learn through a simple phone call.

Instead of requiring a smartphone, a learning app, complicated AI interfaces, or strong mobile data, Vani brings the learning interaction to something many people already know how to use: **a phone call**.

A learner can call Vani, ask questions naturally, learn through conversation, and receive structured revision notes after the call.

Our goal is simple:

> **Make learning accessible to someone who is currently locked out of it.**

---

## The Problem

Modern AI has made learning more powerful, but access to that learning still depends on technology.

Many learning experiences assume that the learner already has:

- A smartphone
- Reliable mobile data
- Familiarity with apps and technology
- The ability to type and formulate prompts
- Comfort with English or other interface-first communication
- The ability to use a device without getting distracted

But not everyone has these advantages.

Someone may only have a basic phone.

Someone may struggle to navigate technology.

Someone may understand concepts better in their own language.

Someone may live in an area where mobile data is unreliable or expensive.

Someone may deliberately use a keypad phone to avoid the distractions of smartphones while still wanting access to AI-powered learning.

These people are not necessarily unwilling to learn.

**They can simply be locked out by the interface through which learning is delivered.**

---

## Our Approach

Vani changes the interface.

Instead of asking the learner to come to an AI application, Vani brings the learning experience to a familiar technology:

### A phone call.

The learner calls Vani and talks naturally.

There is no complicated AI interface to understand.

No prompt engineering is required.

The learner can ask questions, request explanations, ask for examples, and continue the conversation just like they would with another person.

After the conversation, the system processes the transcript and generates structured revision notes.
